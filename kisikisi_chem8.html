📋 Material Review-Kisi-Kisi

  • Acids, Alkalis, and pH Scale: Acid pH <7, base pH >7, neutral pH =7, acid-base indicators (litmus paper), and comparing acidity/alkalinity using pH values.
  • Chemical Reactions of Acids: Reaction with reactive metals (producing salt + hydrogen gas), reaction with bases/alkalis (neutralisation producing salt + water), and reaction with carbonates (producing salt + water + carbon dioxide gas).
  • Solubility & Salts: Soluble and insoluble salts (e.g., Copper(II) sulfate solubility), naming salts, and applications like CO₂ absorption in space using sodium hydroxide.
  • Exothermic & Endothermic Reactions: Energy transfer to/from surroundings, temperature changes, enthalpy change (Delta H), and real-world examples (combustion, respiration, thermal decomposition, dissolving salts).
  • Bond Energy & Activation Energy: Bond breaking (endothermic / absorbs energy) vs bond making (exothermic / releases energy), activation energy definition, and energy calculations (Delta H).
