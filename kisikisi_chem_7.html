📋 Material Review Blueprint (Kisi-Kisi)

  • States of Matter & Particle Model: Arrangements, movement, compression, kinetic energy, and properties of solids, liquids, and gases.
  • Changes of State: Melting, freezing, boiling, evaporation, condensation, and deposition. Cooling effects of evaporation and temperature vs particle motion.
  • Gas Behavior & Diffusion: Pressure, temperature-volume relationship, particle diffusion speeds based on molecular mass/density, and food coloring in water experiment.
  • Elements, Compounds, & Mixtures: Definitions of pure substances, elements, compounds, chemical formulas (e.g., H₂O, C₆₀ Fullerene), and separation techniques (magnetic separation, filtration, evaporation).
  • Chemical Symbols: Chemical symbols for common elements (e.g., Ca, Fe, Ne, Si).
