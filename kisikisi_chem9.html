📋 Material Review Blueprint (Kisi-Kisi)

  • Properties of Metals & Non-metals: Malleability, ductility, conductivity, mercury as liquid metal, aluminium oxide protective layer, and copper applications.
  • Alloys & Molecular Structure: Definition of alloys, brass (copper + zinc), bronze (copper + tin), and why alloys are harder than pure metals (disrupted atomic arrangements).
  • Reactivity Series & Displacement Reactions: Reactivity order (K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > C > Zn > Fe > H > Cu > Ag > Au), reaction with acids/water, and displacement reactions.
  • Corrosion & Physical vs Chemical Changes: Rusting of iron (requires water and oxygen), word equation for rusting, and differentiating physical changes (e.g., ice melting) from chemical changes.
  • Rates of Reaction & Catalysts: Five factors affecting reaction rate (concentration, temperature, surface area, pressure, catalyst), collision theory, activation energy, and catalyst graphs.
